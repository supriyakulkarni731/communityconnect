About Community Connect+

Community Connect+ is an upgraded social-impact platform that brings together elderly people, orphanage organizations, volunteers, donors, healthcare providers, and nearby support services — all under one roof.

Our mission: privacy, dignity, and the power of small acts. Every contribution earns Kindness Points and builds a stronger community. Our new Smart Community Care Network adds healthcare, elderly well-being, and orphanage support — integrated with our original five modules.
