About Community Connect

Community Connect is a community support platform designed to connect people who need assistance with people who want to help. Our mission is to encourage volunteering, share useful resources, and make community support easier to coordinate.

We believe in privacy, dignity, and the power of small acts. Every registration, every food donation, every volunteer hour earns Kindness Points — our way of recognizing your community spirit.
